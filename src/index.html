Welcome to the Fumosite!

What is this website?

This is a small one-page site dedicated to Fumos which are plushies of a unique style and rarity. Most Fumos are of various characters from Touhou Project, though there are additional ones from other franchises.

In short, official Fumos are designed by the doujin circle ANGELTYPE and manufactured/sold by the Japanese company Gift. Gift only ships their products domestically in Japan, however international buyers can still purchase official Fumos through the AmiAmi store.

Where can I purchase a Fumo?

Through official channels, Gift/AmiAmi occasionally posts pre-order runs for a certain set of Fumo characters. You can check their website to see if there are any Fumos available for pre-order.

The Fumo I want is unavailable or too expensive! Are there alternatives?

Unofficially, yes! There is a whole market for bootleg and custom-made Fumos for those who wish to explore alternatives. The Fumo design is not solely Gift nor ANGELTYPE's trademark, so you can easily look for ones that you may like.

Alternatively, there are also resellers who can provide previously-released Fumos. Check this website to learn more about them!
